**Held-out exposures** use rows 1-14 with sky, operator and τ frozen; background, affine, u, z, zD and stripes are refitted on the held-out
pixels themselves (`train.py:697-767`); then stripes are re-solved in closed form on the residual with no prior and added (`train.py:791-807`).

**Scorer, `--level rate`** (`score_paired.py:607-648`, `:742-747`): calibrated-unit predictions × C = rate SCI / cal SCI per pixel (C = flat /
PHOTMJSR, exact where both are finite); count-rate predictions are used as they are; the scorer's offset is a constant in rate units.
Consistent with the model: a cal-trained prediction includes its background in MJy/sr, and C converts sky and background alike.

**Flags (implicit, duplicated, inconsistent, or tied to an unstated convention)**

- A1. *Count-rate sky has per-filter and per-detector units* (F15 states per filter only). The photometric conversion (PHOTMJSR, which differs
  between detectors of one channel and between filters) is not an input. The throughput gauge (row 8) forbids ḡ from carrying a per-filter
  constant, so the conversion goes into the sky's spectrum (and, between detectors, into g_det, whose amplitude is capped at 0.3 rms log g).
  Where two filters overlap in wavelength (F150W/F200W edges, any medium filter inside a wide one, an HST filter against a NIRCam filter)
  one sky spectrum cannot carry two conversions. A joint count-rate fit across instruments is therefore inconsistent by construction.
- A2. *Absolute flux scale of a multi-instrument fit = exposure-count-weighted compromise.* The throughput gauge averages over all training
  exposures; with two instruments whose calibrations differ by 1-3 per cent, the sky's scale is set by how many exposures each contributes.
- A3. *Sky zero level tied to medians that contain sky light.* bkg0 is the median of the data in the cut (`data.py:248`); the pedestal gauge
  makes the background constants average to these medians, so the sky's zero point is "the median pixel of each cut", not the true zero.
  See D1.
- A4. *Flux convention default R = 0* (row 4): the documented 2× sky flux (F41) remains the default behaviour.
- A5. *Telescope diameter used twice with two rules*: warm start per instrument, blur gauge always 6.5 m. **Fixed** (E2).
- A6. *Data-unit step sizes from one mean over all exposures*: background, pattern and stripe steps and the pre-pass background step use
  mean(sigbar) over every exposure (`train.py:294`, `:441-451`, `:710-713`); the sky step uses the mean of σ_b over bands (`train.py:493`,
  `tiles.py:166`); the stripe prior divides by mean sigbar of the batch (`model.py:829`). Mixing short- and long-wavelength channels, filters
  and (later) instruments in one scalar.
- A7. *σ_b is called band noise but is an image contrast* (Starts table). Every rule that compares the sky to "the noise" (positivity penalty
  `train.py:213-214`, bright patches `train.py:66-67`, relocation `sky.py:694-718`) uses it.
- A8. *Item relocation level uses σ_b averaged over bands* (`sky.py:717`): mixes per-filter units in count-rate mode.
- A9. *Random variability fields at step 0* (Starts table): small, but the fit does not start from the state the warm starts assume.
- A10. *Dead factors*: `CHAN_PIX` (`data.py:18`), `area_ext`, `pixar_a2` are loaded and never used by the model. In cal mode α = 1 relies on
  the pipeline convention that `_cal` SCI is a flat-fielded surface brightness (so the AREA extension must not be applied); this is right
  for JWST and HST but is not written down anywhere in the package.
- A11. *Item width cap*: widths are sqrt(floor² + exp(2 l)) with l capped at log(max width), so the cap is sqrt(floor² + max²), slightly
  above `--item-max-width` (`sky.py:465-467`). Cosmetic.
- A12. *Units string* `"cal: MJy/sr, as SCI of the _cal file"` is written for every non-rate prediction (`cli.py:384-385`), whatever the input.

## B. Learned terms that can hide an error

"Before" = shown in the log or `metrics.json` at the end of a fit before this audit. "Now" = added by this audit in
`train.nuisance_sizes` (`train.py:809-896`), logged by `cli.main` and written to `metrics.json["nuisance_sizes"]`; held-out closed-form stripe
sizes go into each held-out record. The report is read-only (test 3 in `tests/test_audit_fixes.py` checks that the state and the random
stream are untouched).

| Term | Bound or prior | What it can absorb silently | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fractional noise floor f_c | softplus, **no bound by default** (`--noise-floor-max 0`) | any error proportional to the signal: flux scale, units, PSF core | yes (value, warning above 0.2) | yes |
| Noise scale e^h per (detector, filter) | h ∈ [−3, 3]: sigma × 0.05 to × 20 | a global misfit of a detector-filter pair (it took over in the count-rate gain run: e^h = 10.8) | **no** | yes, every pair |
| Huber δ = 3 | fixed | bright-pixel misfit (weight falls as 1/\|z\|) | config only | — |
| Per-exposure background (constant + 5 shape terms) | no prior; pedestal gauge on the constants' mean | diffuse light at scales of the cut, kernel far wings, sky zero point | mean constant per filter only | rms and max of (constant − median)/sigbar and of the shape terms /sigbar, for training, validation, held out |
| Detector pattern (8 × 8 per detector) | zero mean per detector, no prior | fixed-pattern residuals, but also any sky structure common to the dithers on that detector | **no** | rms / median sigbar per detector |
| Learned stripes (NIRCam rows and columns) | L2 prior 1e-3 / sigbar², zero mean over used rows / columns | source misfit along rows / columns | **no** | rms and max / sigbar per split |
| Closed-form held-out stripes | **none**; solved on the held-out residual | any residual row / column structure of held-out exposures, in the score itself | **no** | rms / sigma per held-out exposure (`closed_form_stripes`) |
| Affine matrix M_e and translation t_e | Gaussian prior on M (σ = `--affine-prior`); t free (mean projected) | astrometric and colour-dependent centroid errors | held-out values in prediction files only | rms and max per split (matrix; translation in mas) |
| Gain u_e | N(0, 1) × τ_g_exp (EM-updated) | per-exposure flux-scale errors (also of the sky, on held-out exposures) | training only, as τ × rms | raw and in log g, per split |
| Kernel code z_e, distortion code zD_e | N(0, 1) × τ (codes capped at 1.5) | per-exposure PSF and distortion errors | training only, output space | rms per split |
| Throughput fields g_det, g_ep | τ capped at 0.3 rms log g | flat-field and detector-sensitivity errors; in count-rate mode the detector-to-detector conversion | τ table | τ table + "at cap" flags |
| Distortion fields D_static, D_drift | τ capped at 0.03 arcsec | WCS errors | τ table | τ table + "at cap" flags |
| Variability scales (validation rule) | likelihood-ratio check at 2 sd; caps | an axis kept with no Occam pressure follows the validation gradient to its cap (F37) | τ table, ablation z | + "at cap" flags |
| Pixel gain map | gauge projection only; no prior, no smoothness | pixel-scale residuals, including PSF errors at fixed dither positions | gauge residual only | rms and max of log G per map |
| Non-linearity (≤ 20 per cent) | tanh bound | flux-dependent misfit (PSF core errors on bright sources) | **no** | maximum fractional change over 1 to 10⁴ sigbar per channel |
| Kernel far wings (norm radius 0) | none beyond the taper | a flat pedestal of the kernel trades with the background (F41) | **no** | fraction of the kernel integral beyond 0.5, 1 and 2.5 arcsec per filter, and the integral |
| Filter pair shift | none | registration error per (detector, filter) | yes | — |
| Item smooth grid (optional) | positivity penalty on its band images | background and diffuse light | only via the sky's 5th percentile | not added |

Not added (would need a forward pass over data, which the "no change to optimisation" rule does not forbid but which costs time on a GPU run):
the held-out loss with each nuisance switched off, i.e. how much each term actually lowers the score. That is the measurement that would
show a term hiding an error, and is recommended (finding 8).

### Cosmetic

`data.py:17` (detector-to-channel map for NIRCam only; others become their own channel, which is acceptable), `data.py:18` (`CHAN_PIX`,
unused), `model.py:255-256`, `:308-309` (stripe arrays 2,048 wide: harmless while stripes stay NIRCam-only; ACS columns reach 4,096),
`cli.py:57` (`--pix-default 0.031`, only a fallback tile size), `fieldop/__init__.py:1` docstring ("JWST/HST"), `data.py:199` (`PIXAR_A2`).

### Smallest interface an instrument must supply

Per instrument (a small description, no code):
1. `name`, `aperture_diameter_m`, and an optional `kernel_start(λ) → (σ, wing fraction)` for ground-based seeing.
2. `bandpass(filter) → (λ, T)` and the response type (photon-counting).
3. `dq_reject(dq) → bool mask` (its own bit meanings, saturation included) and its own ignore-bits.
4. `detector_terms`: which additive terms apply (stripes rows/columns, amplifier quadrants), and their array sizes.

Per exposure (from the reader):
5. `chips()` → list of (detector id, channel id, detector shape) — one cut per chip.
6. `read(chip) → sci, sigma, usable mask` (sigma from ERR, a variance plane, or read noise + gain).
7. `pixel_to_sky(cols, rows) → (ra, dec)` with the full distortion, differentiable or with a Jacobian.
8. `to_sky_units` → factor (scalar or per pixel) from data units to the common sky unit (f_ν surface brightness), and `level ∈ {surface
   brightness, counts per pixel}` (the latter makes the model apply the pixel area and, optionally, the pixel gain).
9. `mjd`, `exposure_time`, `filter`, `instrument`.

Globally, the spectral domain and the filter-curve input grid should come from the union of the bandpasses (with a margin), not constants.

## D. Faint end and depth (by reading only)

S/N here = signal per exposure per pixel over its pipeline sigma, below 1 in the regime asked about.

| # | Mechanism | Expected sign and rough size | Measurement that settles it |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Pedestal gauge on data medians (A3): the background constants average to the median of each cut, which includes faint galaxies, wings and intracluster light | sky zero point **low** by the median astrophysical surface brightness of the cuts (per filter); for the item sky, which cannot go negative, the faintest areas then sit below every representable sky and the misfit goes to background and noise terms | synthetic field with a known smooth positive component (constant plus an intracluster-like profile) on a known background: recovered minus true sky in the faintest decile; on SMACS, sky level in blank areas against the official mosaic |
| D2 | Item positivity (c = exp(lc)): a non-negative estimate of a flux with Gaussian error | **positive**: at zero true flux the mean of max(F̂, 0) is 0.40 × the flux error of that item; the log parametrisation also makes items approaching zero slow (gradient ∝ c), so blank-sky items keep part of their seed | noise-only synthetic with flat background: mean recovered sky in blank regions against steps |
| D3 | Item seeding floor c_min and the clamp of the smoothed warm start at c_min (`sky.py:478-489`) | **positive** where the warm start is below c_min (blank sky after median subtraction); c_min = 1e-3 × the median peak coefficient, usually well below the noise | the same blank-sky test with the floor set to 0 |
| D4 | Grid positivity penalty `--sky-neg 1` on band images / σ_b (`train.py:213-214`) | **positive** on noise-dominated nodes, small: σ_b is an image contrast (A7), larger than the noise in crowded fields, and the penalty is a mean over nodes | blank-sky synthetic with `--sky-neg 0` against 1 |
| D5 | Preconditioner (bright nodes move up to 41 × faster) + importance sampling (half the patches drawn around bright peaks) + a fixed step budget | faint regions stay close to the warm start, which is PSF-blurred and median-subtracted: faint compact sources **too extended, peaks low**; total flux roughly that of the warm start | recovered/true flux and size of injected faint sources against steps, with `--sky-precond 0` and `--is-frac 0` as controls |
| D6 | Per-exposure quadratic background over the whole detector (on a cut of about a third of a NIRCam short-wavelength detector it is close to linear on the cut), 8 × 8 detector pattern, item smooth grid | diffuse light at scales comparable to the cut is absorbed: **negative**, up to the full amplitude of structure smoother than the cut, less where dithers move the sky across the detector | inject a smooth gradient / intracluster profile into the synthetic field; recover its amplitude |
| D7 | Kernel far wings with norm radius 0 (A4) | wing light and background trade: sky fluxes **high** (documented about 2 × on SMACS) and diffuse light around bright sources ambiguous | the new wing-fraction report; synthetic with known wings, `--kernel-norm-radius` 0 against 2 arcsec |
| D8 | Learned noise scale e^h and floor (B) | a misfit elsewhere that inflates σ also down-weights faint data; with h < 0 faint data are over-weighted; no direct bias of the mean, but the stated depth is wrong | held-out χ² with pipeline sigma against model sigma, per S/N bin (the package logs the former now) |
| D9 | Item relocation (off by default): items with peak below 0.5 σ_b moved to positive residual | **negative** for faint diffuse light (items leave it) | relocation on against off on the D6 field |
| D10 | Blur floor (one sky tent) and item floor (0.4 pitch) | no flux bias; peak heights of unresolved faint sources limited by the floor | none needed beyond the existing synthetic recovery |
| D11 | Validation rule pruning variability axes | neutral for the faint end (axes are chosen on all pixels); no sky bias expected | — |
| D12 | Masks: strict DQ == 0, patches need ≥ 15 per cent valid, cuts need ≥ 2,000 valid pixels | no faint-end bias in the mean; fewer edge pixels | — |

There is no uncertainty for the sky anywhere in the package (no posterior, no Fisher estimate, no ensemble beyond the optional two
cross-fitting views): "honest uncertainty" is not yet a product.

## Ranked findings

1. **Count-rate mode has no photometric conversion input, so its sky has per-filter and per-detector units.** Evidence: `model.py:652-656`
   (α only), F15, gauge `model.py:481-488`. Consequence: a count-rate joint fit of two instruments (or of overlapping filters) cannot fit one
   sky; the error goes to the spectrum, g_det, and then to the noise terms — the family of yesterday's failure. Fix: per-exposure factor
   `to_sky_units` (PHOTMJSR; HST PHOTFNU and nominal area) applied in `render`/`finish`; small code change, refit.
2. **Sky zero point tied to data medians that contain sky light** (`data.py:248`, `model.py:809-815`). Consequence: faint diffuse light
   biased low, and an item sky that cannot represent the region below the median; every new instrument adds its own medians. Fix: an
   explicit sky-zero gauge (e.g. background constants referenced to a sigma-clipped mode of blank pixels, or to an external zodiacal model),
   measured on D1's field; small code, needs the measurement.
3. **Noise scale e^h (× 0.05 to × 20) and an unbounded noise floor can absorb any units error; h was not reported.** Evidence:
   `model.py:608-611`, `cli.py:183-185` default 0; `RATE_HELDOUT_DIAGNOSIS.md` (e^h = 10.8). Now reported (B). Fix: default bounds (floor
   ≤ 0.2, |h| ≤ log 2) with a warning like the floor's, and fail a run whose held-out input-noise χ² exceeds its training value by a set
   factor; trivial code, changes the optimisation (hash).
4. **Spectral domain and filter-curve input grid are constants** (`sky.py:18`, `bandpass.py:18`). Consequence: blue, ultraviolet and
   mid-infrared filters share end coefficients and get an all-zero filter input — silent wrong colours once HST blue filters, GALEX or IRAC
   are added. Fix: derive both from the union of bandpasses (and K from the span); small code; NIRCam default unchanged if kept as a flag.
5. **Absolute flux scale of a multi-instrument fit is an exposure-count-weighted compromise** (`model.py:300`, `--gauge-ref-inst -1`). Fix:
   require an explicit reference instrument when more than one is loaded and log the other instruments' mean γ; trivial.
6. **Loader is JWST-specific** (C, "blocks"): extension names, first SCI only, detector name, `svo_id`, filter sort, 2,000-pixel minimum,
   rate naming, PHOTMJSR in the scorer. Fix: the interface in C; moderate (a reader per instrument, no model change).
7. **One global DQ ignore mask across instruments with different bit meanings** (`data.py:242`). Consequence: HST fill-value pixels kept by
   `--dq-ignore-bits 2`. Fix: per-instrument DQ policy (interface item 3); trivial.
8. **Held-out scores are flattered by terms fitted on the held-out pixels themselves**: closed-form stripes with no prior
   (`train.py:791-807`) plus background, affine, u, z, zD (`train.py:697-767`). Their sizes are now reported. Fix: fit held-out nuisances on
   one half of interleaved blocks and score the other half (as the validation already does); moderate; changes held-out scores, not the fit.
9. **Kernel far wings trade with the background by default** (norm radius 0; `render.py:129-132`; F41: about 2 × sky flux on SMACS). Now
   reported (wing fraction). Fix: make `--kernel-norm-radius` (about 2 arcsec) the default for joint fits; refit.
10. **Detector pattern shared by all filters in calibrated units** (`model.py:254`, `:592-594`). Consequence: a dark-like residual cannot
    be one pattern across filters with different conversions; misfit goes to backgrounds and noise. Fix: pattern per (detector, filter), or
    in count-rate units converted by `to_sky_units`; small.
11. **Faint-end positive biases of the item sky** (D2, D3) and **under-convergence of faint regions** (D5). Fix: measure first (blank-sky and
    injection tests); then consider a signed residual component (the smooth grid at a finer pitch) and per-region step normalisation.
12. **Epoch axes switch on for every instrument when any instrument extends the time span** (`data.py:414`, `model.py:358-375`). Fix:
    normalise t per instrument, or require the span within an instrument; trivial.
13. **Data-unit step sizes, the stripe prior and the sky step use one mean over all exposures or bands** (A6, A7, A8). Consequence: with
    channels or instruments of different sigma, some rows step too fast or too slowly. Fix: per-row scales (sigbar per exposure, σ_b per
    band); small; changes the hash.
14. **Held-out nuisance Adam is not row-masked** (`train.py:746`, `:764` use `opt.step()`, training uses `step_rows`, F24): rows absent from a
    batch keep moving on momentum. Fix: `step_rows` there; trivial; changes the default hash (held-out rows are in the state). Not fixed
    because it is a policy difference not stated as intended either way.
15. **Starting state differs from the warm starts' assumptions** (A9: random 5 mas distortion, 0.02 log-g throughput pattern, 0.3 kernel
    code at step 0). Fix: zero-initialise the last layers of g_det, g_ep, D_static, D_drift, h_det, or start their τ at 0; trivial; changes
    the hash.

### F1. The zero level of the sky (`--sky-zero {median,masked}`, default `median`; `--sky-positivity-margin`, default 0)

**Convention.** A constant sky and the backgrounds of the exposures of one filter are one free direction per filter; the data cannot fix it.
`masked` fixes it by saying that the sky is zero on the pixels the model calls source-free:

```latex
\frac{1}{|T_f|}\sum_{e \in T_f}\bigl(b_{e0} - r_e\bigr) = 0,
\qquad
r_e = \operatorname*{median}_{p}\Bigl\{\, d_{ep} \;:\; p \text{ valid},\;
\bigl|\,(g_{1.5} \ast S_e)_p\,\bigr| < 0.3\,\sigma_{ep} \Bigr\}
```

T_f: training exposures of filter f; b_e0: constant background term; d: data; S_e: the current model's rendered sky (through the response, before
the background) on the exposure's pixels; g_1.5: Gaussian of 1.5 detector pixels; sigma: pipeline sigma. r_e is re-evaluated at fit progress 0,
0.1, 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 (`--sky-zero-at`) and the gauge is applied at once; between those points the existing gauge projection keeps
the mean. `median` (today) is r_e = median of all valid d_ep.

Why the data and not the residual: a level tied to d - S moves with any constant layer of the sky, so it is satisfied by every split and fixes
nothing; with backgrounds left free (`--pedestal-gauge 0`, development run) positivity rectifies the noise and a layer of +0.89 noise units grew
in the sky while the backgrounds fell from 5.19 to 4.08. Why symmetric in S: S carries part of each pixel's own noise, and a one-sided cut
removes the pixels with positive noise (development run: background 0.09 low). Why smoothed: unsmoothed, the cut also keeps the pixels near
faint sources where noise pulled S down (development run: +0.07 to +0.09 high). Several instruments: every exposure is referenced to its own data on the pixels
that the one shared sky calls empty, so the statement "sky = 0 there" is the same for all instruments and every new instrument adds no new
zero point (the per-filter mean gauge is unchanged; a filter shared by two instruments shares one gauge, as today).

The positivity margin (needed for the targets, see below): positivity acts on sky + p_b instead of the sky,

```latex
p_b = m \cdot \operatorname*{median}_{\text{covered nodes } n}\Bigl(\sum_{e,p} w_{epn}\,\sigma_{ep}^{-2}\Bigr)^{-1/2},\qquad m = 3
```

(w: bilinear splat weights of the warm start, so the bracket is the noise of the warm-start coadd at one node; 2.5 at 8 exposures and 1.1 at
32 on the known-truth problem). Grid: the soft penalty acts on (s + p)/sigma_b. Items: the item sum is s + p (seeded with the layer), the sky
returned is items - p.

**Measurements** (known-truth problem, `tests/truth/TRUTH_REPORT.md` section 6 for the full table; 2 noise realisations per row, ideal-noise
units; errors in brackets from the realisations and from the 25 correlated positions):

**Question 1: why the signed sky is 1.6 to 2.1 times the ideal noise.** Not the resolution element: with apertures of 0.035, 0.07, 0.14 and
0.28 arcsec, each in its own ideal noise, the ratio is 1.59, 1.92, 1.21, 1.96 (grid, 8 exposures) and 0.99, 1.58, 1.75, 2.34 (grid, 32), items
up to 3.3 at 0.28 arcsec: it does not fall toward 1 for wide apertures. Not under-convergence: 800 steps instead of 400 (grid, 8 exposures, seed 0)
give 2.10 against 1.91 at 0.07 arcsec and 3.72 against 2.12 at 0.28 arcsec. Something else, growing with scale; candidates, not separated:
the per-exposure quadratic backgrounds against the sky's large scales (dithers +-0.3 arcsec on a 4.4 arcsec detector) and a sky with about
as many free nodes in the covered region as half the data at 8 exposures (one freeze of the background's higher terms would separate them; it
needs a hook). Sampling error of each rms about 15 per cent at 0.07 arcsec, larger for wider apertures (fewer independent positions).

**Question 2: does positivity still bias the faint end once the zero level is right?** Yes. With `masked` (background within 0.03 to 0.05 of
the truth) the raw empty sky is +0.44 (grid) and +0.23 (items) noise units at 8 exposures, its scatter about the mean 0.05 to 0.66 (clipped),
and the total offset stays at +0.4 to +0.6 noise units at 32 exposures: rectified noise, constant in noise units. The margin removes it.

**Approximations and shortcuts.** Single tile only (`--sky-zero masked` refuses several tiles). The threshold 0.3 and smoothing 1.5 pixels
were chosen with grid fits on noise seed 0 at 8 exposures (4 development runs, listed in TRUTH_REPORT section 6), and seed 0 is in the tables;
the margin was tried at one value. Each re-evaluation renders every training exposure once (5 times per fit). The margin's noise scale is that
of the warm-start coadd, not of the fitted sky (grid node noise is larger), so some nodes may still touch -p. Item sky with a margin: the sky
is items - p inside the sky domain and zero outside it, a step of -p at the domain edge seen by the edge pixels' response. The held-out
exposures keep free backgrounds, as before. The "total" uses the true background (as in the truth report). Two realisations per row; one items
fit took 267 s of wall time (two fits ran at a time).

**Not done.** No real-data fit; no test with two instruments; no separation of the two candidates of question 1; no regularisation of the
signed sky at the node scale; the masked reference's bias from faint unseparated light was measured (0.034 to 0.047) but not reduced.

### F2. Count-rate input in sky units (`--rate-units {dn,sky}`, default `dn`; `--rate-photom FILE`; `--nuisance-steps {mean,exposure}`, default `mean`)

**Convention.**

```latex
\hat d_{ep} \;=\; G_p\sum_{q} w_{fq}\, e^{\gamma_{epq}}\;
\frac{a_{ep}}{a^{\mathrm{nom}}_{e}\; C_e}\;\bigl(K_{epq} \ast s_q\bigr)
\;+\; B_{ep}
```

s: sky in MJy/sr (the calibrated fit's unit); a_ep = |det dOmega/dxi| the model's pixel area (header WCS plus learned geometry, F15);
a_nom: nominal pixel area (PIXAR_A2 of the header read); C_e: conversion in MJy/sr per DN/s, PHOTMJSR of the matching `_cal` file with
`--rate-wcs cal` (its header is already read for the positions), else `--rate-photom` {"DETECTOR/FILTER": C} (optionally
"DETECTOR/FILTER/area" for a_nom; without either, a_nom = the cut's header pixel area). The run stops if an exposure has no conversion.
1/(a_nom C) is a per-exposure constant (`rate_kappa`); gamma (throughput) and G (pixel gain) stay learned and free to correct it.
Starting values: the warm-start splat divides the data by a_ep x rate_kappa (in `make_sky` and after the registration pre-pass), so the
sky, its band noise, the item seeding, the positivity margin and the sky step size all start in MJy/sr; backgrounds and sigma stay in DN/s.
`--nuisance-steps exposure` (new, off by default) makes the Adam step of the background and stripes per exposure (lr x sigma_e instead of
lr x mean sigma, audit A6); without it a count-rate fit and a calibrated fit of the same field take different background steps whenever the
conversions differ between exposures.

**Check (a), synthetic** (`tests/test_rate_units.py` run as a script, 150 steps, kernel warm start 150 steps; five (detector, filter)
pairs with conversions 3.5, 2.3, 3.7, 2.2, 0.42 on 3 detectors and 3 filters, relative pixel areas 0.97 to 1.03): warm-start band fluxes equal to 1e-6;
after the pre-pass to 3e-4. After the fit, band flux rate/cal 0.9948, 0.9991, 0.9951 (target 2 per cent); held-out chi-squared 1.940 against
1.971, ratio 0.984 (target 3 per cent). Without `--nuisance-steps exposure` the same run gave 1.035, 1.020, 0.986 and 0.977. With
`--rate-units dn` the warm-start band fluxes differ from the calibrated ones by factors 27.9, 44.5 and 160 (the conversions and areas the sky
then absorbs). Both fits are far from converged (held-out 1.94 to 1.97 against a floor near 1); the unit test runs 30 steps and holds the
held-out ratio to 6 per cent (4.1 per cent in a development run of it).

**Check (b), real exposures, no fit** (`tests/check_rate_photom.py`, 12 local `_rate` files with their `_cal` files; median and robust
scatter, 1.4826 x median absolute deviation, over 3.5 to 3.7 million pixels each) of rate x PHOTMJSR / (a / PIXAR_A2) / cal:

equivalent to, but not the same code as, the model's per-pixel Jacobian. The synthetic check has one constant header area per exposure and

is off by default); the pattern step (per detector, lr x mean sigma) and the stripe prior keep the single mean (A6 otherwise unchanged);
no change to the scorer (`score_paired.py`).

### F3. What should become the default

* `--sky-zero masked`: yes, now. It removes the dependence of the zero point on source light (background error 0.19 -> 0.03 to 0.05), the
  offset no longer grows with depth, faint fluxes improve, the held-out score is unchanged, and it is the convention that carries over to
  several instruments. It changes the default state hash and costs five full predictions per fit.
* `--sky-positivity-margin 3`: for the deepest-sky product (faint end, empty-region statistics), yes; as the general default, not yet: it is
  the only setting that meets the offset and depth targets, but it buys them with noise at 1.6 to 1.9 times the ideal and a held-out score 5
  to 7 per cent above the floor at 8 exposures. It should become the default together with a fix for question 1 (a node-scale prior or a
  background-sky separation), measured on this problem.
* `--rate-units sky` (with `--nuisance-steps exposure`): yes for every count-rate fit: the `dn` sky has per-filter and per-detector units
  and cannot be shared by two instruments; the conversion is an input and a starting value, nothing else.
