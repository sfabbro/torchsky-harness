# E2 with learned kernels (13 Nov 2026)

Kernels: fieldop run `fieldop_smacs_fix_200_s0` (JWST NIRCam, SMACS), mean code at the filter pivot, centre of detector NRCB3 (F090W, F150W, F200W) or NRCBLONG (F277W, F356W, F444W), detector frame, pixels of 0.031" / 0.063". Errors are max |error| / peak pixel of the reference, worst of 3 sub-pixel positions. Kernel table: 16 samples per pixel, +-32 px (`results/cache/learned_tables.npz`).

**How the kernel is evaluated (reproduced from the code and the state file, FieldOperator not rebuilt):** `k(v) = exp(l(v, h)) * taper(r) / Z`. `v` = offset in the detector frame in arcsec (the package rotates sky offsets by the exposure's R_e; we tabulate in v directly). `l` = FiLM network on the log-radius x angle encoding (r0 = 0.0025", 12 harmonics). `h` = pivot code + 1e-6 x (wavelength and detector terms: the run pruned both) (the per-exposure term, 0.059, is left out). `dil` recomputed from the state: 1 (blur floor not binding; sharpest class M_ref 5.40e-4 vs floor 5.33e-4 arcsec^2). `Z` = the package's nested-level quadrature (rotation of a real exposure; spread over exposures < 1e-4). Taper 8.5" to 10". The kernel is NOT pixel-integrated separately: fieldop reads the continuous sky at pixel centres, so the learned kernel is the whole "pixel-in-kernel" response and no box was added. Operator is a correlation, so the table is flipped for rendering.

**Verdicts against 1e-3 of the peak.**
1. Kernel fit by Gaussians on the specified pyramid: **fails by a factor of 500 or more** (positive 0.67 to 0.83, signed 0.50 to 0.78; rms 3e-3 for both). The smallest Gaussian (0.35 px) is wider than the kernel core. Even a pyramid starting at 0.08 px reaches only 0.31 to 0.37 positive and 0.06 to 0.19 signed. Signed is better than positive, as with the stand-ins, but not usable.
2. Truncation: **fails for the large source** (re 32: 1.7e-2 to 5.3e-2 at +-32 px, 4e-2 to 0.13 at +-8 px) because 52 to 66 percent of the kernel integral lies beyond +-32 px. Small source (re 2): passes at +-32 px for F090W to F277W (3.8e-4 to 8.2e-4), borderline for F356W, F444W (1.0e-3, 1.1e-3); at +-16 px only F150W, F200W pass.
3. Local sampling at 2, 4, 6 samples per pixel: **fails** except re 32 with n = 6 on the long-wavelength kernels (F277W 2e-4, F356W and F444W 8e-4; reference uncertainty about 5e-4 there). Short-wavelength re 32 gives 1.1e-3 at n = 6 and 1.2e-3 to 1.8e-3 at n = 4. re 8 and re 2 fail at n <= 6 everywhere (re 8: 1.0e-3 to 2.3e-3 at n = 6; re 2: 2.7e-3 to 4.5e-3). Short-wavelength kernels need n = 8 for re 8 (3e-4 to 4e-4); re 2 still fails at n = 8 (1.7e-3 to 2.0e-3). Long-wavelength errors are NOT monotone in n (n = 8 worse than n = 6). Cause suspected: the kernel peak, 0.4 px wide at 0.125 px off the origin, is hit differently by each grid; not isolated.
4. Point source: **fails** (nearest sample 0.18 to 1.1; cubic interpolation from n = 8: 6.5e-2 to 0.17; from n = 16: 1.8e-2 to 3.3e-2 worst of 3 generic offsets, 5e-4 to 3e-3 at the other two).

**Non-negative everywhere:** yes (minimum 5e-6 to 1.5e-5 of the peak value at +-32 px; the network is an exponential). Structure: no rings (azimuthal median profile falls monotonically, with a shoulder at 3 to 6 px), but strong angular structure: radial streaks with 0.1 to 0.25 dex azimuthal rms at 1 to 16 px, spike pixels (more than 2 x the ring median) hold 13 to 25 percent of the flux at 2 to 8 px; dynamic range 4.4 to 4.8 dex from peak to 32 px. Peak at (0.25, 0.125) px SW, (0.125, 0.0625) px LW; centroid offset 0.04 to 0.25 px.

**Caveats and approximations.** (1) Relative kernels: core FWHM is 0.023 to 0.024" in all six filters (a physical PSF grows with wavelength); the blur floor ties the sharpest class to one 0.04" sky tent, and the sky grid carries the rest of the blur. These are sharper than the physical PSF (narrower than the pixel box), and the model's effective response is the kernel convolved with the tent. (2) Pixel scales 0.031 and 0.063 taken from the task, not the run's header medians. (3) Pivot wavelength, mean code; the run's wavelength nodes differ by < 1e-5 in log kernel; detector and wavelength variation was pruned (tau 1e-6) so the "centre of the detector" kernel is the kernel everywhere. (4) Kernel mass: point-sampled tables at 2 to 8 per pixel carry 0.2 to 18 percent different mass from the converged one, so all rendering tables are scaled to the converged +-32 px integral (Richardson of 16 and 32 per pixel); 4/px fit tables are not. (5) Fits at 4 samples/px (as e2) by streaming QR instead of full QR, errors given on that grid (relative to its own peak, 0.36 to 0.88 of the true peak) and on the 16/px grid (relative to the true peak; the figures in the table). (7,4,half spacing) of e2 not repeated; the extra pyramid (sigma0 0.08 px, fitted on 16/px) and the larger stencil (8 levels x 49) are additions. Flux captured by Gaussians 1.05 to 1.29 (1.74 for the extra pyramid, LW): Gaussian tails beyond +-32 px. (6) References: re 2, re 8: kernel split smoothly at 8 to 12 px, inner part Richardson (16, 32), outer (8, 16); re 32: Richardson (8, 16) only (n = 32 does not fit in memory). Estimated reference error from plain 32 vs reference: <= 1.3e-4 (SW) and 2.1e-4 (LW) for re 2 / re 8; re 32 <= 7e-5 (SW), <= 5e-4 (LW). Sources sampled with 6x6 sub-samples near the centre in the references, not in the tested method. (7) "Full" kernel in the truncation test: +-32 px at 8/px plus the rest to 10" at 1 sample/px. (8) Point-source offsets 0.137/-0.291, -0.382/0.213, 0.471/0.059 px (not on the 1/8 px grid, unlike e2). (9) Z of the package quadrature vs the continuous integral of the same function: 1.001 (SW) and 1.007 to 1.011 (LW); the level grids sample the sharp LW core at 0.16 px.

**Looked wrong / surprising.** (a) 52 to 66 percent of the integral beyond 1" (0.35 to 0.40 beyond 4" for the short-wavelength kernels) is far more halo than a real NIRCam PSF has; likely a gauge or sky-background trade-off of the relative kernel, not physics. (b) The log's final blur moment (5.69e-4) differs by 5 percent from my recomputation from the state (5.40e-4); both are above the floor, so `dil` = 1 either way. (c) Kernel peak off the pixel centre by up to 0.28 px.
